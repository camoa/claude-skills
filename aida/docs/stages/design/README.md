# Design

Design is the third stage. It turns the contract and the research into work orders, checks them
against the contract, and closes when you approve them.

## Pages, in reading order

1. [Start design](start.md): the command, when design does not start, and the run mode.
2. [What design reads](inputs.md): research findings, plays, guides, the framework recipe, and
   lookups.
3. [What a work order is](work-orders.md): the fields, served and owned, size, the interface.
4. [How an order is proved](proof.md): the five proof kinds and how design picks one.
5. [Where an order's verify lines come from](verify.md): recipe and research lines, and your
   approval.
6. [Owned files, shared files and surfaces](files.md): what an order may write, and pages.
7. [Answer each reuse candidate](reuse.md): reuse, extend, supersede or decline.
8. [Change a work order](change.md): update, merge, remove, and a design that already closed.
9. [Run the design check](check.md): what holds the close, and what only informs.
10. [Critique and close the design](close.md): the owners, the critics, the close, and what
    comes next.

Before design: [Research](../research/README.md). After design: [Implement](../implement/README.md).
