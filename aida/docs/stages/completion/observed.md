# What a model observed

Some criteria were judged by a model that looked at a page through a browser. Completion puts
each one to you, and you say whether that look stands.

## What completion shows

One question per criterion. For each row the model judged, it shows the done-when sentence, the
surface and viewport, and the verdict. It names two images: the one taken before the build and
the screenshot after. Open both. A row that says the page is as it was is judged between the two.

## Your answer

Say yes or no per criterion. Every such criterion needs an answer, or the close refuses. A no
closes the task only on your reason, one sentence, the way a failed review does.

The record keeps each answer. The pull request body says beside each criterion that a model
judged it from a screenshot, and whether you accepted it.

## Why it works this way

- A model looked, not a person. You decide whether its look counts before the work ships.
